  Serving as a peer reviewer for Acta INFOLOGICA (ACIN), a peer-reviewed scientific journal managed by the Department of Informatics at Istanbul University. The journal has been publishing since June 2017 and focuses on the concepts of data, information, and knowledge based on information and communication technologies. ACIN aims to provide a scientific publication environment for studies in informatics and interdisciplinary research. The journal is published twice a year in June and December, applying a double-blind peer review process.
